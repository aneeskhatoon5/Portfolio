ABOUT ME

Securing insights through analysis and samples

I am Anees Khatoon, a Forensic Chemistry and Toxicology graduate from Liaquat University of Medical & Health Sciences with a CGPA of 3.87/4.00 and a Silver Medal (2nd position among 67 students). My work sits at the intersection of advanced analytical instrumentation, sample extraction methodologies, and toxicological screening, and I care about building reliable analytical workflows that hold up under strict quality standards, not just in a lab.

My research on functional nanomaterial synthesis was published in 2026. I have completed practical laboratory internships at the Punjab Forensic Science Agency (PFSA), Sindh Police Forensic Division, Chemico-Bacteriological Laboratory, and the LUMHS Department of Molecular Biology & Genetics for molecular techniques.

I enjoy the full lifecycle of an analytical problem: sample collection and matrix purification, method optimization using advanced tools like GC-MS, LC-MS/MS, and HPLC, extracting precise measurements, then writing up the findings clearly enough that someone else can act on them.
